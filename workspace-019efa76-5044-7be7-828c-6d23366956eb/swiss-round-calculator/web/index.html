備註：Note:

為保守起見，即使存在和局，仍使用無和局的二項式分佈公式，因為和局會使積分分佈更為分散，從而減少頂層的選手數量——因此，即使有和局發生，將其作為下界也是安全的。

The no-draw binomial formula is used for conservative reason even when draws exist because draws spread the point distribution more, reducing the number of players at the top — so it's safe to use as a lower bound even when draws occur.

在假設勝、和、負結果的概率時，這裏簡單地採用了二項式分佈，而不是複雜的多項式分佈或常態分佈。

The binomial distribution is taken for simplicity reason instead of the complex multinomial distribution or the complicated normal distribution in assuming the probability of the results of wins, draws, and losses.

這裏假設計分制使用了標準的1-0.5-0制。分出勝負的對局與和局的得分不一樣的計分制，或負局得分不是0的，則不限於此公式。

The point-scoring system of 1-0.5-0 is assumed. This formula does not apply to point-scoring systems where the total score of a decisive game is not the same as the total score of a draw game, or if there is a non-zero loss score.

黃氏計分法有以下好處：
Wong system has the following advantages:

  1. 降低比賽最後兩輪消極和棋的動機，而不是為了再平衡先後手的差異。
    It does not mean to rebalance the difference of colour whilst it lowers the motivation for negative draws in the last two rounds.
  2. 增加比賽後段出現棋手因確定將獲得一定名次而故意缺席的成本。
    It increases the cost of deliberate absences for players certain of certain ranks in later rounds.
  3. 把常負但出席率高的棋手從底層分離出來，不致他們排名低於早早退出或經常缺席的棋手，及在配對時使他們免於對碰，因為常缺席的棋手有可能在較後輪次再次缺席。
    It segregates the players who often lose but have high attendance from the bottom group so that they rank not lower than the players who withdraw or are absent often, and it avoids them meeting during pairing because frequent absentees tend to be absent again in later rounds.
  4. 在保持同等輪數的情況下，使棋手排序分得更精準，令實力相當的棋手更大可能對碰。
    It makes the ranking more precise with the same number of rounds, making games of closer match.
  5. 由於可以細分棋手高低，可減省為確保排出具極強說服力的冠亞軍而加諸於最少輪數之上的額外輪次。
    As it can rank the players more precisely, extra rounds on top of minimum rounds for the sake of ensuring strong convincing champion and runners-up can be saved.
  6. 鼓勵棋手積極爭勝的同時限制合謀造假的得益在安全水平。
    It encourages players to strike for a win while limiting the gain from collusion at a safe level.

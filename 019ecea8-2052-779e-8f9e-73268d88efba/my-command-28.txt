The reason for I asked you to consider using iframe at the very beginning was because in much earlier stage of development, the alpha and beta versions, I hosted the pages(the legal documents and Wong system pages on their own) with different fonts from the fonts on mobile web and react native app on purpose. At that time, I tried to use Get Element to fetch the pages directly from GitHub. The result was inconsistent and unstable. The legal pages always displayed in the fonts as specified but the Wong system page sometimes displayed the source page font, sometimes the fonts specified for the react native and the web view font bounced. Then, I tried iframe which always delivered the fonts of the source page. So, I changed the pages on GitHub and the project codes to have the same fonts. We can't inject styles cross-origin for iframe but WebView injection is prone to have issues with dependencies in RN during expo start. Inlining as base64 makes the package too big for transferring to mobile Phone Expo Go (time out).

The functions page and the Wong system page have no dates in the content. The verification may not be as simple as the legal pages. But they are less likely to be updated as frequent as the legal pages.

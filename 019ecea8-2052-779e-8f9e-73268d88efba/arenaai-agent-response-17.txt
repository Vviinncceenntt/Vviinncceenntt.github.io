## Why your earlier "exp:// in Safari → Expo Go → timeout" happened

Expo Go fetches **two** things after the `exp://` handoff:
1. A manifest from `http://your-ip:8081/` (small JSON, ~2 KB)
2. The bundle from `http://your-ip:8081/index.bundle?...` (large JS, ~5 MB on first build)

For request **1** to succeed, the iPhone must reach `http://your-ip:8081/`. You just confirmed Safari can't reach that — so Expo Go can't either. The "timeout" you saw was step 1 failing, not the bundle. That's why **nothing ever appeared in the Metro window**: the request didn't get there.

`/status` and `/` go through the same TCP socket, so if one works and the other doesn't, it's not a routing issue — it's that **the connection drops between them**. Causes:
- Wi-Fi adapter sleeping between requests
- Router AP isolation kicking in (some routers do it sporadically)
- IP changed mid-session

A reboot of your PC + iPhone Wi-Fi off/on resets all of these. Try that before going further.
